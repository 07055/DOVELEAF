## What Happens When Tailored Clothes Touch Water?

The fundamental difference between laundry and dry-cleaning is **water**. Water is a polar molecule that swells natural hydrophilic fibres like wool, cashmere, and silk:

1. **Differential Shrinkage:** The outer wool fabric, the synthetic lining, and the internal chest canvas shrink at three completely different rates when wet. This causes the infamous "bubbled lapel" effect, rippled front panels, and misshapen shoulders that can never be ironed flat again.
2. **Loss of Natural Fibre Oils:** Water and harsh detergents strip natural lanolin from wool and sericin from silk, leaving the fibers brittle, dull, and prone to tearing.
3. **Seam Puckering & Dye Bleed:** Water immersion relaxes tensioned tailor stitching, causing permanent puckering along armholes and waist seams.

---

## The Science of Non-Aqueous Dry Cleaning

Professional dry-cleaning does not mean cleaning with dry powders. It means cleaning garments in specialized, non-water solvent fluids:

* **Zero Fibre Swelling:** Because dry-cleaning solvents contain no water, natural protein fibers do not swell, shrink, or felt.
* **Superior Oil & Grease Dissolution:** Body oils, sebum, perfumes, food greases, and atmospheric pollution are non-polar compounds. Solvents dissolve these lipids effortlessly without mechanical scrubbing.
* **Preservation of Garment Architecture:** The delicate inner structure of lapels, shoulder pads, and collars remains completely undisturbed and crisp.

---

## Not All Dry Cleaning is Created Equal

To preserve high-end garments over decades, a dry-cleaning plant must operate with disciplined operational engineering:

* **Continuous Solvent Distillation:** Using pristine, crystal-clear distilled solvent to eliminate redeposition of grey soil and foul chemical odors.
* **Specialized Pre-Spotting Boards:** Spotting tables equipped with steam, vacuum suction, and targeted chemical agents for tannins, proteins, and inks.
* **Precision Tension Steaming:** Finishing on utility presses and dummy formers that restore the original three-dimensional silhouette without flattening fine fabric naps.
