Running a successful laundry business requires far more than quality cleaning. Customers need to discover your business, understand your service tiers, and feel confident trusting you with their everyday garments, suits, household linen, or institutional bulk laundry.

Behind the scenes, owners also need practical digital tools to record sales, track branch performance, and manage daily activity.

Our **Marketing & Systems Services** at **Doveleaf Drycleaners & Advisory** support laundry businesses in these two connected areas: **attracting and communicating with customers**, while **putting digital tools in place to organize business information and routine processes**.

## The Common Challenge: Marketing Without Measurement

A typical commercial laundry or neighbourhood laundromat often faces a painful disconnect:

1. **Unmeasured Social Media Effort:** The business posts sporadically on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok, but struggles to turn vanity likes into actual inquiries, booked pickups, or loyal repeat customers.
2. **Fragmented Record Keeping:** Daily sales and order intakes are recorded across scattered paper counter-books, lost receipts, or unlinked spreadsheets.
3. **Blind Decision Making:** Without clear tracking, business owners cannot reliably monitor month-on-month sales trends, staff productivity, customer drop-off rates, or true branch profitability.

A coordinated approach to marketing and systems bridges this gap. When marketing activities and operating systems talk to each other, laundry businesses communicate more consistently, attract better clients, and maintain clear, verifiable records of their day-to-day operations.

## Part 2: Our Systems Setup & Training Services

Marketing attracts the customer; operating systems ensure the customer is served profitably and returns repeatedly. We install and configure practical digital tools around the services, prices, workflows, and reporting needs of your laundry business:

### 4. POS Installation & Customisation
* **Tailored System Configuration:** Setup of modern laundry Point of Sale (POS) software matched to your physical space and counter workflow.
* **Category & Price List Mapping:** Structuring your service catalogue (per-piece dry cleaning, wash-and-fold per kg, commercial bulk linen, shoe restoration) with clear pricing and delivery lead times.

### 5. Google Sheets & Business Tracking
* **Daily Sales & Intake Dashboards:** Customized Google Sheets for daily order logging, revenue tracking, and cash-in-till reconciliation.

### 6. Staff Training & Handover

## How Our Advisory Engagements Work

We employ a clear 5-stage advisory framework to ensure measurable improvements for your laundry business:

```
[ 1. Assess ]  --->  [ 2. Plan ]  --->  [ 3. Implement ]  --->  [ 4. Train ]  --->  [ 5. Review ]
```

1. **Assess:** We evaluate your current services, target customer profile, existing marketing channels, and counter tools to identify operational bottlenecks and growth opportunities.
2. **Plan:** We agree on an actionable roadmap outlining the right marketing campaigns, content calendar, and software setup tailored to your budget.
3. **Implement:** Our team coordinates your marketing creative, installs the POS system, formats your Google Sheets trackers, and customises your service catalogues.
4. **Train:** We guide the business owner, cashiers, and supervisors through practical, scenario-based training sessions on the new systems.
5. **Review:** We monitor agreed key indicators—such as customer inquiries, daily logged sales, and operational error rates—to refine and optimise the workflow.
